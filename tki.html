Conflict Mode Assessment (TKI)

Understand how you typically respond when your wishes differ from someone else's.

30 Questions ~7 minutes Results shown instantly Responses logged to Google Sheets
Based on the Thomas–Kilmann Conflict Mode Instrument (TKI)
Purpose: The TKI describes conflict behaviour along two dimensions — assertiveness (satisfying your own concerns) and cooperativeness (satisfying the other's concerns) — producing five modes: Competing, Collaborating, Compromising, Avoiding and Accommodating. There are no right answers; each mode is useful in the right situation.
Instructions: Consider situations where your wishes differ from another person's — with managers, colleagues, family or friends. For each pair, choose the A or B statement that is more characteristic of you. Even if neither fits well, pick the one you'd be more likely to use.
